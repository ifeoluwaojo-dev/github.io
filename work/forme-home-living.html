01 / THE CONCEPT

A calmer way
to browse.

How can a shopping experience feel considered rather than crowded? I created FORME., a fictional homewares brand with a cohesive visual identity, warm editorial photography and straightforward product navigation.

This is an independent portfolio build — not a paid client project or real retailer. All prices, products and orders are illustrative.

02 / THE EXPERIENCE

Details that
do something.

The site combines interface design with useful interactions that make browsing feel fluid across desktop and mobile.

Product discovery

12 demo products, five categories, text search, price filtering and sorting.

Wishlist & cart

Save favourites, add products, change quantities, and maintain state with localStorage.

Individual product pages

Photo gallery, illustrative material and dimension details, and related product suggestions.

Responsive experience

Mobile layout, clear navigation, keyboard-friendly controls and reduced-motion support.

03 / FROM IDEA TO INTERACTION

A complete journey,
minus the payment.

FORME. demonstrates a realistic front-end shopping journey, including a browser-only simulated checkout with a sample discount code, shipping calculations, form validation and confirmation state.

The demo is clearly labelled: no actual payment is collected, and no customer details are transmitted. The project runs as a static website on GitHub Pages.
