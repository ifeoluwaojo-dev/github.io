01 / THE FOUNDATION

Complexity needs
structure.

My bachelor's-degree background is grounded in research, engineering analysis, and structured written communication. Across academic projects, I worked with literature, technical assumptions, alternative approaches, and practical constraints.

The transferable skill is not any one engineering topic — it's the ability to break an unfamiliar problem into pieces, test ideas against evidence, and communicate findings in a way others can review.

03 / THE TRANSFER

Research skills travel
well.

Whether evaluating a technical design or a written response, the most valuable habits remain consistent:

  • Define the question and the limits of what is known.
  • Review evidence instead of assuming the first plausible claim is correct.
  • Compare alternative approaches and the trade-offs between them.
  • Communicate reasoning and uncertainty in clear language.

I bring this mindset to work in research, analysis, quality checks, and AI response evaluation.
